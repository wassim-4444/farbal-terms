Privacy Policy - FARBAL R9-1

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This policy explains what the FARBAL R9-1 desktop application accesses and stores on your computer.

Information the application accesses

  • Your video files that you explicitly select for processing and upload.
  • Platform access tokens for TikTok and YouTube, stored only in the local auth folder of your own computer.
  • Basic metadata you type yourself, such as titles, descriptions, privacy level and hashtags.

Information the application does not collect

  • No analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising identifiers.
  • No upload of your videos, tokens, or files to any server operated by the developer. Videos go directly from your computer to TikTok and YouTube.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data with third parties.

Storage and retention

Access tokens and generated clips stay on your device until you delete them. Removing the application and its auth folder removes them. You can also revoke access at any time from your TikTok and Google security pages.

Third-party platforms

TikTok and YouTube process your uploads under their own privacy policies. This application is not responsible for how those platforms handle your content.

Children

The application is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to the developer through the contact information shown in the application listing.
